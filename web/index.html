Scope — read this before using the numbers

This selector covers ONE verified slice of the 9.23.12.3 tables: SPF No. 1/No. 2, non-structural sheathing, 2-ply lintels supporting roof and ceiling only, in residential buildings where supported joist spans are ≤ 4.9 m and truss spans ≤ 9.8 m. The Code's 3-ply, roof-plus-storeys and structural-sheathing tables could not be verified from a readable public source, so they are not here — a tool that guesses lintel spans is worse than a smaller tool that doesn't. In 38 × 64 loadbearing walls, lintels must be at least 50 mm deeper than the tabled size and no longer than 2.24 m.
